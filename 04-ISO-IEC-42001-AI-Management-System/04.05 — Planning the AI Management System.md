# 04.05 — Planning the AI Management System

## 1. Purpose of Planning the AI Management System

Planning the AI Management System (AIMS) establishes the structured approach through which the organization determines what it needs to achieve, what risks and opportunities must be addressed, what actions are required, and how those actions will be controlled and evaluated.

Effective planning connects:

* Organizational context.
* AI objectives.
* AI risks and opportunities.
* Applicable requirements.
* AI governance responsibilities.
* Operational controls.
* Resources.
* Performance measures.
* Continual improvement.

Planning should ensure that the AIMS is deliberately designed rather than developed through disconnected governance activities.

---

## 2. Planning as a Core AIMS Activity

Planning provides the foundation for coordinated AI governance.

A simplified planning cycle is:

```text
Organizational Context
        ↓
AI Requirements
        ↓
Risks and Opportunities
        ↓
AI Objectives
        ↓
Actions and Controls
        ↓
Resources
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Monitoring
        ↓
Review and Improvement
```

Each element should support the effectiveness of the overall management system.

---

## 3. AIMS Planning Principles

AIMS planning should be based on several principles:

1. **Risk-based** — planning reflects identified AI risks.
2. **Objective-driven** — activities support defined AI objectives.
3. **Integrated** — AI planning aligns with enterprise planning.
4. **Accountable** — responsibilities are clearly assigned.
5. **Evidence-based** — decisions are supported by relevant information.
6. **Proportionate** — governance effort reflects AI risk and complexity.
7. **Adaptable** — plans can change as AI technology and circumstances evolve.
8. **Measurable** — planned outcomes can be evaluated.
