# 04.16 — Management Review

## 1. Introduction to Management Review

Management review is a formal governance activity through which top management evaluates the continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the AI Management System (AIMS).

The review provides an opportunity for leadership to examine whether the AIMS continues to support:

* Organizational objectives.
* AI governance requirements.
* AI risks and opportunities.
* Stakeholder needs.
* Applicable obligations.
* Continual improvement.

Management review should be based on relevant evidence rather than informal discussion alone.

---

## 2. Purpose of Management Review

The primary purpose of management review is to enable top management to evaluate the overall performance and direction of the AIMS.

Management may use the review to determine:

* Whether the AIMS remains appropriate.
* Whether objectives are being achieved.
* Whether risks are adequately managed.
* Whether controls remain effective.
* Whether resources are sufficient.
* Whether changes are required.

Management review therefore connects operational evidence with strategic governance decisions.

---

## 3. Top Management Responsibility

Management review is a leadership responsibility.

Top management should provide appropriate direction concerning:

* AI governance.
* Resources.
* Risk management.
* AI objectives.
* Performance expectations.
* Improvement priorities.

Management review should not be treated solely as an administrative exercise performed by the AI governance team.

---

## 4. Management Review and the AIMS

Management review forms part of the AIMS performance-evaluation and improvement cycle.

A simplified relationship is:

```text id="h6m2vr"
AIMS Operation
      ↓
Monitoring & Measurement
      ↓
Internal Audit
      ↓
Risk & Performance Information
      ↓
Management Review
      ↓
Management Decisions
      ↓
Actions / Improvement
      ↓
AIMS
```

This allows leadership to determine whether the management system continues to operate as intended.

## 5. Management Review Frequency

Management review should occur at planned intervals.

The appropriate frequency may depend on:

* Organizational size.
* AI system criticality.
* Risk profile.
* Number of AI systems.
* Regulatory environment.
* Significant changes.
* Previous management-review results.

Higher-risk AI environments may require more frequent management attention.

---

## 6. Planned Management Review

Management reviews should be planned rather than conducted only when a problem occurs.

Planning should consider:

* Review timing.
* Required participants.
* Inputs.
* Performance information.
* Audit results.
* Risk information.
* Decisions required.
* Follow-up from previous reviews.

A planned approach improves consistency and accountability.

## 31. Management Review of AIMS Suitability

Suitability concerns whether the AIMS remains appropriate for the organization's purpose, context and AI activities.

Management may consider whether:

* The AIMS supports organizational strategy.
* The AI governance model remains appropriate.
* AI policies remain relevant.
* The scope remains suitable.
* Existing processes reflect the current AI environment.

Changes in organizational strategy or AI adoption may require adjustments to the AIMS.

---

## 32. Management Review of AIMS Adequacy

Adequacy concerns whether the AIMS has sufficient capability and resources to fulfill its intended purpose.

Management may evaluate:

* Available resources.
* Competence.
* Processes.
* Controls.
* Technology.
* Documentation.
* Governance capacity.

An AIMS may be well-designed but inadequate if the organization lacks the resources required to operate it effectively.

---

## 33. Management Review of AIMS Effectiveness

Effectiveness concerns whether the AIMS achieves its intended results.

Management may consider:

* Achievement of AI objectives.
* Risk-treatment effectiveness.
* Control performance.
* Audit results.
* Incident trends.
* Stakeholder feedback.
* Performance indicators.

Effectiveness should be assessed using evidence rather than the mere existence of policies and procedures.

---

## 34. Suitability, Adequacy and Effectiveness

These three concepts can be distinguished as follows:

| Concept       | Key Question                                              |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Suitability   | Is the AIMS appropriate for our organization and context? |
| Adequacy      | Does the AIMS have sufficient capability and resources?   |
| Effectiveness | Is the AIMS achieving its intended results?               |

Management review should consider all three dimensions.
