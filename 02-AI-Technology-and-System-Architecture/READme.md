# 02 — AI Technology and System Architecture

**Purpose:** Give the GRC professional enough technical knowledge to understand what is being governed.

This chapter provides the technical foundation required for an AI Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) professional to understand how modern AI systems are built, deployed, integrated, secured, and governed.

The objective is **not to turn the GRC professional into an AI engineer**. Instead, the objective is to provide sufficient architectural and technical understanding to evaluate:

* AI risks
* AI security controls
* AI governance requirements
* AI data flows
* AI system dependencies
* AI architecture
* AI third-party services
* AI security boundaries
* AI control implementation
* AI assurance and compliance

# GRC Perspective

The central concept of this chapter is:

```text
AI Technology
      ↓
AI Architecture
      ↓
AI Assets
      ↓
AI Data Flows
      ↓
Threats
      ↓
Risks
      ↓
Security Controls
      ↓
Governance Requirements
      ↓
Evidence
      ↓
Assurance
```

The GRC professional does not need to build every AI component.

However, the GRC professional should understand **what the components do, how they interact, where risks arise, where controls should be implemented, and how those controls can be assessed**.

# Key GRC Question

Throughout Chapter 02, the primary question should be:

> **"What is this AI technology, how does it work within the system architecture, what can go wrong, and where should governance and security controls be applied?"**

This question provides the foundation for connecting AI technology with **Governance, Risk, Compliance, Cybersecurity, Privacy, and Assurance**.
