# Chapter 3 — AI Governance Operating Model

## Purpose

Chapter 3 establishes the **enterprise AI Governance Operating Model** required to translate AI governance principles, policies, risk requirements, and strategic objectives into a functioning organizational structure.

The chapter explains how an organization designs, implements, operates, measures, and continuously improves its AI governance capability.

It focuses on **who governs AI, who makes decisions, who owns AI risks and systems, how governance bodies interact, how decisions are escalated, and how the operating model becomes embedded into enterprise operations**.

---

# Key Principles

The AI Governance Operating Model developed in this chapter is based on several principles.

### 1. Clear Accountability

Every material AI system, risk, control, and decision should have an accountable owner.

### 2. Risk-Based Governance

Governance requirements should be proportional to the potential impact and risk of the AI activity.

### 3. Separation of Duties

Material decisions should have appropriate independence between development, risk assessment, approval, and assurance.

### 4. Executive Oversight

Material AI risks should receive appropriate executive and Board-level oversight.

### 5. Lifecycle Governance

Governance should operate from AI ideation through retirement.

### 6. Business Integration

AI governance should be integrated with existing enterprise risk, security, privacy, compliance, data, and internal control processes.

### 7. Evidence-Based Governance

Governance decisions should be supported by appropriate evidence.

### 8. Continuous Improvement

The operating model should evolve as AI technology, regulations, risks, and organizational requirements change.

### 9. Proportionality

Low-risk AI should not be governed in exactly the same way as high-risk AI.

### 10. Business Enablement

Governance should enable responsible AI adoption rather than unnecessarily obstruct innovation.

# Chapter 3 Key Takeaway

The central principle of Chapter 3 is:

> **AI governance becomes effective when governance structures, accountability, decision rights, risk authority, policies, controls, processes, reporting, assurance, and continuous improvement operate together as an integrated enterprise capability.**

AI governance should therefore not be viewed simply as a collection of policies or committees.

It is an **operating model** that defines how the organization makes decisions, assigns accountability, manages AI risk, exercises oversight, demonstrates compliance, and enables responsible AI adoption.
