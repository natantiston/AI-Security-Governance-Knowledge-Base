# Chapter 01 — AI Security Governance Foundations

## Overview

Chapter 01 establishes the foundational knowledge required to understand, design, implement, operate, and continuously improve **AI Security Governance** within an enterprise environment.

The chapter explains AI Security Governance from its fundamental concepts through enterprise strategy, governance structures, risk management, controls, assurance, operating models, and strategic roadmapping.

It establishes the conceptual foundation for the remainder of the AI Security Governance knowledge base.

---

## Chapter Objectives

By completing Chapter 01, the learner should be able to:

* Explain the meaning and purpose of AI Security Governance.
* Distinguish AI Governance from AI Security Governance and Cybersecurity Governance.
* Understand the relationship between Responsible AI, Trustworthy AI, Secure AI, and Ethical AI.
* Explain why AI requires dedicated governance considerations.
* Understand AI Security Governance principles and objectives.
* Identify the major stakeholders involved in AI Security Governance.
* Understand AI Security Governance operating models.
* Apply risk-based governance principles to AI systems.
* Understand AI Security Governance throughout the AI lifecycle.
* Connect AI Security Governance with enterprise GRC.
* Understand AI security policies, standards, controls, and procedures.
* Establish AI Security Governance accountability and decision rights.
* Understand AI Security Governance assurance, metrics, and reporting.
* Design an enterprise AI Security Governance program.
* Develop a strategic AI Security Governance roadmap.
* Understand how AI Security Governance evolves as AI technology and risks change.
