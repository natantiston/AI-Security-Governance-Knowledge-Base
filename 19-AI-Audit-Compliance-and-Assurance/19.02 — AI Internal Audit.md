# 19.02 — AI Internal Audit

## Part 1 — AI Internal Audit Foundations, Governance, Independence, Scope and Core Concepts

### 1. Definition of AI Internal Audit

AI Internal Audit is an independent and objective assurance and advisory activity that evaluates whether an organization's AI governance, risk management, and control processes are appropriately designed and operating according to defined criteria.

AI Internal Audit may examine:

* AI governance
* AI risk management
* AI security
* AI privacy
* AI lifecycle controls
* AI model governance
* AI third-party dependencies
* AI regulatory compliance processes
* AI operational resilience
* AI control effectiveness

AI Internal Audit is an organizational assurance function. It should not automatically be treated as a statutory audit unless a specific legal or regulatory requirement establishes such an obligation.

---

### 2. Purpose of AI Internal Audit

The purpose of AI Internal Audit is to provide independent evaluation and assurance concerning defined AI governance, risk, and control objectives.

Its purposes may include:

* Evaluating control design
* Testing control operation
* Identifying control weaknesses
* Assessing risk-management processes
* Evaluating governance structures
* Assessing compliance processes
* Identifying recurring weaknesses
* Supporting accountability
* Reporting material issues to appropriate governance bodies

**AI Internal Audit ≠ Management of AI Risk.**

Management owns AI risks and controls; Internal Audit evaluates them independently.

---

### 3. AI Internal Audit vs AI External Audit

AI Internal Audit is performed within or on behalf of the organization's internal assurance function.

External audit is performed by an independent external organization under its own engagement terms and applicable professional requirements.

| Dimension | AI Internal Audit | AI External Audit |
|---|---|---|
| Organizational relationship | Internal assurance function | External provider |
| Primary purpose | Internal assurance and improvement | Defined external assurance objective |
| Scope | Organization-defined | Engagement-defined |
| Reporting | Management / board / audit committee | Intended external users |
| Independence | Organizational independence required | External independence requirements may apply |
| Methodology | Internal audit methodology | Applicable external methodology |

The exact distinction depends on the engagement and applicable professional framework.

---

### 4. AI Internal Audit vs AI Assessment

An AI assessment evaluates a defined condition against specified criteria.

AI Internal Audit is broader when it includes:

* Formal audit planning
* Independent evidence evaluation
* Testing
* Findings
* Audit conclusions
* Reporting
* Follow-up

An assessment can be one input to an internal audit without itself constituting an internal audit.

**Assessment ≠ Internal Audit Automatically.**

---

### 5. AI Internal Audit vs AI Compliance Review

A compliance review may evaluate whether defined legal, regulatory, contractual, policy, or framework requirements are addressed.

AI Internal Audit may evaluate compliance-related controls while also examining:

* Governance
* Risk management
* Control effectiveness
* Operational processes
* Evidence
* Accountability

**Compliance Review ≠ Complete Internal Audit Automatically.**

---

### 6. AI Internal Audit vs AI Security Audit

An AI security audit focuses specifically on security-related criteria and controls.

Potential areas include:

* Identity
* Access control
* Data protection
* Model security
* Application security
* Infrastructure security
* Monitoring
* Incident response
* Supply-chain security

AI Internal Audit may include these areas when they are within the approved audit scope.

**AI Security Audit ⊂ Possible AI Internal Audit Scope.**

---

### 7. AI Internal Audit vs AI Governance Review

An AI governance review evaluates governance arrangements such as:

* Roles
* Accountability
* Policies
* Committees
* Decision rights
* Risk ownership
* Escalation
* Oversight

AI Internal Audit may audit governance controls but should maintain an independent position rather than becoming the governance owner.

---

### 8. AI Internal Audit vs AI Control Testing

Control testing evaluates whether a defined control is designed, implemented, or operating effectively.

AI Internal Audit may perform control testing as part of an audit engagement.

However:

**Control Testing ≠ Internal Audit Automatically.**

A control test may be performed by management, compliance, security, or another assurance function without constituting an internal audit.

### 54. AI Internal Audit Control Design

* Is the control linked to the risk?

* Is frequency appropriate?
* Is the control sufficiently precise?
* Does the control address the identified failure mode?

**Control Design ≠ Operating Effectiveness.**

* Approved policy exists.

### 399. AI Internal Audit Enterprise Principles

An enterprise AI Internal Audit program should operate according to the following principles:

1. **Internal Audit should remain sufficiently independent and objective.**
2. **Management remains responsible for AI governance and risk decisions.**
3. **Audit scope should be explicit and documented.**
4. **Audit criteria should identify their source and authority.**
5. **Risk-based planning should use defined criteria.**
6. **Control design, implementation, and operating effectiveness should be assessed separately.**
7. **Audit evidence should be relevant, reliable, sufficient, and appropriate.**
8. **Sampling conclusions should remain aligned with the sampling methodology.**
9. **Automated testing should be validated.**
10. **AI-assisted analysis should remain subject to human evaluation.**
11. **Findings should be supported by evidence and defined criteria.**
12. **Root cause should be distinguished from symptoms and contributing factors.**
13. **Corrective-action completion should not automatically establish effectiveness.**
14. **Assurance conclusions should remain within the defined scope.**
15. **Material assurance limitations should be disclosed.**
16. **Internal Audit should not assume management responsibilities.**
17. **External and supplier assurance should be evaluated before reliance.**
18. **Maturity should not be represented as compliance.**
19. **Continuous monitoring should not automatically be represented as continuous assurance.**
20. **Internal audit methodology should evolve as AI technology and risk change.**
